Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how NoteAfloat Labs ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use Maze Escape (the "App").

We respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about how information may be processed when you use the App.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not require users to create an account, sign in, or provide personal information such as their name, email address, phone number, physical address, contacts, photographs, or precise location.

The App is designed to function offline and does not require users to submit personal information to us.

However, the App uses third-party services, including Google AdMob and Firebase, which may independently collect or process certain information from or about your device and use of the App. Such information may include device information, advertising identifiers, app usage information, diagnostic information, crash information, and other technical information necessary for the operation, measurement, security, and improvement of their respective services.

The information processed by these third-party services is subject to their own privacy policies and terms.

2. Advertising

The App is free to download and use and may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob, a service provided by Google.

AdMob may process certain information about your device and interaction with advertisements in order to provide, deliver, measure, and improve advertising services. Depending on applicable settings, laws, and Google's advertising practices, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized.

For more information about how Google handles information in connection with advertising, please review Google's applicable privacy policies and information about advertising technologies.

3. Analytics, Crash Reporting, and App Performance

The App uses Firebase services provided by Google for analytics, crash reporting, diagnostics, and understanding app performance.

These services may process technical and usage information, including information about app events, device characteristics, diagnostics, and crash information.

We use this information to understand how the App performs, identify technical problems, diagnose crashes, improve stability, and improve the App.

We do not use Firebase to create user accounts or require users to provide personally identifying information to us.

4. Internet Connectivity

The core maze gameplay is designed to function without an internet connection.

The App does not require users to create an online account or upload gameplay progress to our servers. The App does not operate its own online user-data storage or user account system.

5. In-App Purchases

The App may offer optional in-app purchases.

Purchases are processed through Google Play's billing infrastructure. We do not directly collect or store your payment card number, bank account information, or other payment credentials.

Information related to a purchase may be processed by Google Play as necessary to complete and manage the transaction.

6. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or directly disclose users' personal information to third parties for our own independent marketing purposes.

However, information may be processed or shared with third-party service providers that support the App, including Google services such as AdMob and Firebase.

These third parties may process information in accordance with their own privacy policies and applicable terms.

We may also disclose information where reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, legal process, governmental requests, enforce our rights, prevent fraud or abuse, or protect the security and integrity of the App.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

We do not maintain user accounts or maintain a database of personally identifiable user information collected directly through the App.

Because third-party services such as Google AdMob and Firebase may independently process certain technical, advertising, analytics, or diagnostic information, their respective retention and deletion practices may apply to that information.

If you have a privacy-related request concerning information processed by a third-party service, you may also need to contact the relevant service provider directly.
